My sister or my wife?

My Sister Or My Wife? theyeshiva.net, essay, Oct 30 2025. Sent by Chayim, Oct 19. · my rank 1 of 5

RECOMMEND, and it now ranks above A. Added as a new option. A to D stand. Permission to reprint: cleared by Mendy, Oct 19.

The sentence a reader does not already believe

“We crave to always be husbands and wives”, but sometimes, for a marriage to survive, “we must become brothers and sisters”. Every marriage piece we have ever run says rebuild the feeling. This one says that when the feeling is gone you stop trying to feel married and fall back on a bond that never needed feeling in the first place.

Avraham tells Sarah to say she is his sister. Two readings in the Rishonim, and out of the second one comes the claim: “At these moments, one must remember that his wife is, in essence, also a sister and that her husband is also a brother.” Marriage as a created union can go flat. Marriage as a shared root cannot. Then the second half turns it on the reader's own avodah.

Sources
Ramban on Bereishis 12VERIFIED Quoted saying Avraham committed “a great sin, inadvertently”, with the Zohar (Tazria 52a) answering it in the next sentence. Accurately quoted. Printed in a GYE newsletter it generates letters. That is a decision to take on purpose, not one to let ship by default.
Likkutei Sichos vol. 20, Baal Shem Tov Al Hatorah, Tanya 18 and 25VERIFIED Footnoted by him, by volume and page. He is the kind of author whose citations ride with his name, so these are not being re-checked.
Against the archive

NEVER RUN Nothing like it in 151 campaigns. Brother and sister as a marriage idea: zero hits. The one “also a sister” neighbour is Vayigash, Dec 26 2025, a member about his actual sister. Last year's Lech Lecha marriage piece was Yechezkel Stelzer, “Look Out a Different Window”, a different argument. “essentially holy” and “ahavah mesuteres”: zero hits.

Why it is worth it

It is the only candidate on this page written for a marriage that is flat rather than one that is fighting, and that is most of our list. Its second half then says the same thing about the reader and the Ribono shel Olam: “But then come the days when you enter into a psychological Egypt, where your inner spirituality is numbed, as you are overtaken by self-centered lusts, beastly cravings, negative impulses, and enslaving addictions.” “Your marriage with G-d seems all but dead.” And the answer, “You are holy not because you feel holy, but because you are essentially holy”.

What is wrong with it

Its second half is a Lech Lecha dvar Torah about addiction, and the issue already has two candidates for that slot. Run whole here and the strongest hashkafa line anyone sent this week is sitting in the marriage column. That is an editorial pick-one, not a build call.
The reciprocity promise. “When you are there for your wife, even when you’re not in the mood for it, an extraordinary energy of love is later returned to you.” To a man whose wife is not coming back around, that is one more thing he did that did not work. Rule 01.
Disclosure, again. “That requires the work of exposing our vulnerabilities, pain, and fears”. One sentence, but it is C's question, flagged Oct 13 and still unruled. Choosing H does not avoid it.
No action steps. It has the subheads and no closing block. That has to be written here.

Your wife is a mirror

Secrets of Love, 28:35 to 45:21, minus the afikoman block · my rank 2 of 5

RECOMMEND. The strongest thing left in the shiur.

The sentence a reader does not already believe

The size of your reaction has almost nothing to do with what she said. It is a burn you have carried since you were three, and she just touched it. So the question after she says something that makes you furious is not what she did. It is “What just happened inside of me?”

The Baal Shem Tov, “every person we look at is a mirror”. Jacobson's reading: what I experience looking at you has very little to do with you. Then the mashal that carries it, “We all have sunburns in our soul” and your wife knows exactly where they are. Worked out through two specific cases he gives: the man who shut his emotions down as a boy and now calls his wife dramatic, and the man who had a controlling parent and hears a request as control.

Sources
Baal Shem Tov, every person is a mirrorUNVERIFIED Said in his name and no sefer named. The idea is standard in chassidus and the printed source has to be found before it goes out under his name. This is the whole load-bearing citation of the piece, so it is not a detail.
The sunburn, the two cases, the pauseVERIFIED Jacobson's own, 38:49 to 43:46. Nothing borrowed.
Against the archive

NEAR MISS Chukas, Jul 4 2025, “Why You Notice Everything Your Spouse Does Wrong” (R' Benzion Shafier). Same territory, different argument: that one is selective attention plus your strength being her weakness. This one says the reaction is an old injury of yours. Fifteen months apart and a different claim, so it is runnable. It should not open by sounding like it.

Why it is worth it

It is the only thing in the shiur that is both news and ours. A reader does not walk in believing his anger is about his own childhood, and once he does believe it the piece has changed something. It is also the mechanism our reader already knows from the other side: a trigger is a trigger. The marriage column has never said that out loud and it is the one place the marriage section and the rest of the newsletter touch.

What is wrong with it

It runs along the edge of therapy and we are not therapists. “Stop going to Shul. Deal with your pain.” is a line we would not print under any circumstances. The piece has to stop at noticing. Pause, be curious, ask what just happened. The moment it tells a man what to do with what he finds, it is out of our lane.
The afikoman passage sits in the middle of this section and has to be cut out of it. 30:08 to 34:51, “whatever me and my wife hide, our children are going to reveal”. To a man whose hidden thing is this, that is a sentence about his children paying for it. Rule 01. Best told piece in the shiur and still out.

B

The garbage bag by the door

Secrets of Love, 46:08 to 49:23. Hard stop at 49:23. · my rank 3 of 5

RECOMMEND as the second piece, and it is the one with the GYE line in it.

The sentence a reader does not already believe

Whether your wife laughs at your mistake or calls it abusive was decided long before you made it. And the reason a man cannot believe his wife loves him is usually that he does not like himself. In his words, “sometimes when you hate yourself, you can’t appreciate that somebody else loves you”.

From a marriage seminar he ran in Monsey. A wife puts the rubbish right against the door so her husband cannot miss it, and “he climbed over the garbage bag” and went to shul. She tells it as a joke. Another woman in the room says “Why is this funny? It’s abusive.” Same story, two verdicts. The difference is that the first woman already knows her husband loves her. Then the turn: giving the benefit of the doubt is not a decision you make in the moment, it is downstream of what you already believe about yourself.

Sources
Havei dan es kol ha'adam l'kaf zechusVERIFIED Avos 1:6. Quoted correctly, used correctly.
The Monsey seminar and the therapistUNVERIFIED Unnamed. Fine, because the story carries itself and nothing hangs on who told it. It should run as a story, not as a cited case.
Against the archive

Why it is worth it

The second half is GYE's actual subject and it arrives inside a marriage piece. A man who does not like himself cannot receive being loved. Our reader is frequently that man. Nothing in the archive has made that connection in the marriage slot.
It is also the best-told story in the shiur that is not ruled out on sensitivities.

What is wrong with it

It must stop at 49:23. What follows is the 20 to 1 compliments ratio, and that ran at Shoftim, Aug 29 2025, cited to Gottman. Running on into it turns a fresh piece into a re-run.
The second woman cannot be the villain. Some wives reading this are in a genuinely hard marriage and read the story the way she did. The piece has to be about the first woman's security, never about the second woman being wrong.

The sentence a reader does not already believe

The parts of yourself you have decided are unsayable are the parts that stay lonely, and ending loneliness is what marriage was for. “if there’s deep things about me that I cannot share with my wife”, that means really we are both lonely.

His argument against the quiet marriage: avoiding every explosive subject looks like peace and is two people being lonely in the same house. The remedy is sharing the hard thing as your own experience rather than as a charge, and the other one holding space instead of running.
