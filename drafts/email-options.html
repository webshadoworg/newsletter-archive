Body opening — settled

“1 new message awaits your response” — not shipping it

Grammar: singular and plural both switch (“1 new message awaits” / “3 new messages await”) and so does the button, so it costs three conditionals.

The bigger problem is “awaits your response.” It tells the member they owe something. These are often people not answering because it already feels like too much, and an email that opens with an obligation is the one they leave unread. It also replaces a name with a counter, so the email stops sounding like Chaim and starts sounding like a ticket queue.

And a count above 1 is only possible if notifications get batched. One email per reply means the line is permanently “1 new message” and the counter earns nothing.

If you still want the count: keep the person as the headline and add a small grey line underneath — “and 2 earlier messages are still waiting” — shown only when it is 2 or more. One conditional, never reads “1 new message,” never demands anything.

“View Message” vs “Read & reply”

Your instinct on View Message was right and I keep going back and forth on it. “Read & reply” asks for two things and one of them is work — for a member who is avoiding the conversation, that is a reason to close the email. A button that only promises to show you something is easier to press, and the reply follows once they are in the chat.

v1 currently shows “Read & reply.” Say the word and it becomes “View Message” — I did not want to add a seventh question over one label.

Brand colors — settled

Both tiles use one purple and one yellow, exact inversions of each other: #624b78 and #f9d772. #624b78 appears 70 times across GYE’s existing branded emails.

Rebuilt as PNG at 110×114 from one shared letterform mask, so they are sharp on retina at any size we use. A vector from the designer is still worth having before the tile ships larger than ~45px.
