Project Kedusha: A Sneak Peek

As a friend of GYE, we want you to be among the very first to hear about a new project we've been quietly building together with Mishor, the organization led by Rabbi Yitzchak Lazerson. It's called Project Kedusha, and the concept is simple - yet, we believe, revolutionary.


Technology is moving faster than ever. New devices, new platforms, new AI tools are emerging constantly - and with them, a flood of questions that families, schools, and individuals are asking right now:

  • I need to use AI for my business - is there a kosher way to do it?
  • My child just got a new device. What's the right filter for this specific situation?
  • Two filtering options came out this month - which is better for our family?
  • My teenager is spending hours on social media. Where do I even start?


At the same time, there are more solutions available than ever before - but they are scattered, hard to find, and difficult to evaluate. Most people don't know where to turn.


Project Kedusha changes that. It's a single place where anyone - no matter their device, their situation, or their level of technical knowledge - can instantly find the right solution, without red tape, without confusion. Describe what you're looking for, and it tells you exactly what to do, or helps you choose between options.


What makes it truly different is Mishor. They are the beating pulse behind the project - constantly tracking every new development in the technology and kedusha space, so that any new solution is immediately reflected in the system. The guidance is always current.


The bigger vision: the more people have access to easy, up-to-date protection and guidance, the fewer people will ever need to turn to GYE for help in the first place. This project isn't just support after the fact - it's prevention. It's building the infrastructure that protects families before problems start.


We're also thrilled to share that we've secured an excellent domain for the project: kedusha.com. The website is currently being built, and it will point you toward:

  • Filtering Resources - guidance for installing effective filters, with expert assistance available worldwide
  • Screen Addiction - help reclaiming time from endless scrolling for meaningful connections and purposeful living
  • Education on Kedusha - materials and curriculums to develop a Torah-true perspective on technology use
  • Ongoing Chizuk and Support - continuous encouragement to maintain high standards in daily technology use
  • Confidential Help for Those Who Struggle - discreet resources for individuals facing challenges with inappropriate internet use
  • Kosher Isle - wholesome entertainment and educational content while maintaining kedusha standards


As Yaakov Nadel, Co-Founder of GYE, puts it: the goal is for technology to serve us, never to enslave us.


What makes this launch especially exciting is that kedusha.com isn't just a website - it's coming in several formats, each designed for a different kind of person and moment:


The website lets you chat and immediately find what you need - type in your situation and get pointed in the right direction, instantly.


A printable guide is nearly ready - a beautifully designed resource that a rav, rebbetzin, or school could hand to someone who needs direction, even offline. We currently have a draft circulating to a select group of reviewers, and the early feedback has been incredibly encouraging.


An AI-powered hotline is planned as well - where someone can call, describe what they're looking for, and be guided to the right resources through a natural voice conversation.


We are targeting a launch by Rosh Hashana - and before we go live, we'd love your feedback. As someone who knows this world deeply, your perspective matters. You're welcome to share thoughts on the concept itself, on the printable guide, or on the website as it takes shape at kedusha.com. What do you think? Is there anything missing? Any situation you've encountered that you don't see covered? Please reply to this email - your input will directly shape the final product.

GYE Connect: Nobody Gets Lost in the Crowd

Picture GYE as a small office serving fifty people.


We would know every one of them. We wouldn't send generic reminders and hope for the best. We'd notice that the man who signed up two weeks ago hasn't come back since. We'd notice the one who's been showing up every day and is probably ready for something deeper. And we'd reach out to each of them personally, with something that actually fit where he was holding.


In an office of fifty, you can't lose track of someone you see every day.


GYE serves far more than fifty. The question driving our work these past few years has been how to give thousands of people the kind of attention you would naturally give fifty. We decided early on that we would not wait for members to figure it out alone and reach out once things got bad enough. We would go to them.


Until now, that meant a welcome sequence: a message offering to talk, then a couple of reminders. And it worked. Many wrote back, and those conversations changed lives.


But it left a silence we could never reach into. Everyone who didn't write back stayed invisible. We had no way to walk down the list, member by member, and see who had quietly stopped showing up, who was struggling, who was one small nudge away from real progress. The people most likely to disappear were exactly the people we couldn't see.


Even the members who did reach out came to us through a system with real limits. We used an off-the-shelf platform and customized it heavily, but every conversation still began with detective work: hunting for what he had told us months earlier, assembling the picture, then starting over for the next person, and the next. Hours that belonged to our members were going into figuring out who our members were.


A year of asking one question


This past year, we gave serious time and attention to a single question: what can the new AI technologies actually do for an organization like ours? We wanted to know where they could genuinely help us reach more people, reach them sooner, and give each one something better than we could give him before.


We explored a lot. Some of it led nowhere, and we moved on. But one answer kept surfacing. AI is strongest exactly where we were weakest: reading through mountains of information and handing a human being the part that matters.


The first major project to come out of that year is now live. We call it GYE Connect, because the connection that takes care of itself in a room of fifty is precisely what we had to build for thousands.


What it does


GYE Connect is the internal system our support team now works from, and we are rolling it out across our entire membership. Its purpose is simple: make sure no member is ever a stranger to us, and no staff member ever walks into a conversation unprepared.


Every person who registers is visible. Our team can go through them one by one and see, at a glance, how someone has been engaging with the site, what he's been working on, what he's been avoiding, and where he's holding right now. The outreach that follows isn't a reminder blasted to a list. It's a specific message to a specific person about his actual situation.


When a member does write in, everything he has ever shared with GYE is already there, gathered in one place and read for us before we read it. GYE Connect uses AI to do the work that used to consume our staff's hours:

  • It pulls together every conversation a member has had with us, no matter which staff member handled it or how long ago.
  • It summarizes those conversations, his forum posts, and his tracking history into one clear picture of who he is and what he's facing.
  • It surfaces the details that matter most, so something small he mentioned eight months ago doesn't get lost.
  • It flags what we might otherwise miss, which is often the difference between a supportive reply and the right one.


A staff member sits down, absorbs a member's full story in under a minute, and spends his time on the part only a human can do.


Following through, not just following up


The real test of support isn't the first conversation. It's everything after.


When we connect a member with a mentor, GYE Connect keeps that thread alive. It brings us back to him weeks later to ask how it's actually going. If we offered him a few mentors to choose from, we find out which one he called, whether the fit was right, and what he needed that he didn't get. That feedback loops back into how we match the next person.


The same holds for the whole journey. Every member is carried on one continuous conversation that never closes, and the system brings him back to us at the moments that matter: an early check-in, again at three months, and again a year on.


We stay with a member until one of two things happens. Either he's doing well and genuinely no longer needs us. Or he's ready to turn around and help the next person walking through the door.


Just the beginning


GYE Connect went live only recently, and we already see how much further it can go. Every week of using it teaches us something: a follow-up that should have come sooner, a detail worth surfacing that we hadn't thought to look for, a member we almost missed. We're building those lessons back into the system as we learn them, and we expect to keep doing that for a long time.


We can't promise anyone that he'll succeed. That isn't ours to promise, and we'd be lying if we did. What we can promise is that every person who comes to GYE will be met by a real human being who knows his story, who checks back when he goes quiet, and who stays with him through the hard stretch. Warm, personal support, for every single member.


That's what gives a person his best chance. Our work now is to make sure not one of them misses it.

Project STEP: Filters, Available to Everyone

We wanted you to be among the first to know about something Guard Your Eyes just launched - made possible by the generosity of a sponsor who believes, as we do, that money should never be the reason someone goes without protection.


Project STEP - Safe Technology, Easy Protection - is a simple, guided way for anyone to get a real filter, installed professionally, on any device. Fill out a short form - five minutes - and a filter specialist takes it from there. They handle the installation, the configuration, the whole setup. No technical background needed on your end.


The filter is GenTech - one of the strongest and most flexible filtering solutions available today.


And here's the part we're most proud of: cost is no longer a barrier. Every applicant is automatically subsidized 50% off the price of the filter. And for anyone who genuinely can't afford even that, the remaining cost is covered too - completely free. Just as Guard Your Eyes has always been free for anyone who needs it, filters now can be too.


We're not writing to ask you for anything. This program is already funded and ready to go. We just wanted you to know it exists - and to ask a small favor: if you know someone who's been meaning to get a filter, or someone for whom cost has always been the obstacle, please pass this along. Sometimes all it takes is one person sharing the right thing at the right moment.

A New Kind of Welcome: AI-Powered Intake at gye.org/start

Getting started with GYE has always meant filling out a form. That's changing.


We're launching a new intake experience at gye.org/start - a conversation, not a form. When someone arrives at GYE for the first time, instead of a list of fields, they're greeted with a simple question: "What are you hoping for?" From there, the conversation unfolds naturally. Demographics are gathered only after the person has shared something, when there's already some trust in the room. The AI knows how to respond based on what someone says - going deeper with someone who opens up, patiently following up with someone who hesitates, and never pushing.


What's remarkable about this approach is where it ends. When someone completes the intake conversation, their profile and full conversation history are already waiting for our staff. And that conversation - the very first thing they shared - becomes turn one of their ongoing support relationship. There is no gap between intake and care.


The experience is built with short, warm, plain language - no therapy-speak - and includes explicit disclosure that a real GYE expert will read the conversation and follow up personally. Launching soon at gye.org/start.
