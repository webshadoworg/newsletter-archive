Chayim's draft

Chayim, Slack, Oct 19. Pasted as text, no link.

Lech lecha read as "go to yourself", carried on Bereishis Rabbah 39:9: Hashem withheld the destination to make the land precious and to reward Avraham for every step.

For

The spine is good and it is not a re-run. The midrash is better than he claimed it was: it says פסיעה ופסיעה, step by step, at our own pasuk.

Against

One line cannot print as written: "not in spite of the falls but through them." That makes the fall the engine of the rise. Needs you or a rav. The Sefas Emes attribution does not hold, and the Vilna Gaon line could not be found in any printed source. No title.

MY PICK

The Dessler lech lecha

From Rafael's Hebrew gilyon · source

Yitzchak's lech lecha, not Avraham's. He had to move off his father's derech to build his own. What you were raised on may not be what works for your struggle.

For

Same thesis as Chayim's draft and properly sourced: Michtav MeEliyahu, volume and page, three places. Dessler is already a house voice in English, not an import. And it carries its own fence, in his words, that this is not פריקת עול but real קבלת עול.

Against

It opens on Yitzchak and spends most of its length in Toldos, so the Lech Lecha hook is one sentence. The להעמקה נוספת appendix has no home in an English dvar Torah and should be cut, not shrunk. Rafael wrote it, so crediting is your call. No title.

The second half of the Jacobson essay

theyeshiva.net, sent by Chayim · source

"You are holy not because you feel holy, but because you are essentially holy."

For

The strongest single line anyone sent this week, and it is aimed directly at our reader: the days when "your inner spirituality is numbed" and "your marriage with G-d seems all but dead." Never run in 151 issues.

Against

It is the back half of the marriage piece. Using it here means splitting one essay across two sections of the same issue, or choosing which section gets it.

MY PICK

Jacobson, "My Sister Or My Wife?"

theyeshiva.net, Oct 30 2025. Permission cleared by you tonight. · source

When the feeling is gone, stop trying to feel married and fall back on a bond that never needed feeling: "we must become brothers and sisters."

For

Rated above everything else on the marriage list. Zero overlap in 151 campaigns, and it is the only candidate written for a marriage that is flat rather than one that is fighting.

Against

No action-steps block, that has to be written. The Ramban is quoted saying Avraham committed "a great sin, inadvertently", answered from the Zohar in the next line, accurate, and it will generate letters. One promise needs softening: that when you are there for her "an extraordinary energy of love is later returned to you."

Why newcomers refuse the 12 Steps

From Rafael's Hebrew gilyon · source

If I am already failing while fighting with everything I have, then surrendering will obviously take the brakes off entirely. The turn: it sounds logical and does not work, against it works and does not sound logical.

The best-written thing in the batch. Nine English campaigns have mentioned the 12 Steps and every one of them is an advert. Not one explains why it works. We have sold the program nine times and never answered the objection that is stopping people.
