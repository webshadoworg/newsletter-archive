From name

GYE Personal Support

No agent name, and nothing that says what GYE is - it tells the member why they are getting the email without saying what it is about. The From line is what sets expectations: “Chaim from GYE” reads like a personal note and invites a reply, which currently dead-ends at no-reply@. It is also the same sender for every agent, so the member’s existing thread and filters keep working when a different person picks up the conversation. At 20 characters it shows in full in an inbox list; Gmail on a narrow phone cuts sender names around 20-25.

The card below says “GYE Personal Support team” under the agent’s name - the word team reads well as a role line in the body but makes the sender name longer than it needs to be, so the From name drops it. Say the word if you want them identical.

From address

no-reply@gye.org  interim

Comes out as soon as members can reply to the email directly. Until then, set Reply-To to the Intercom inbound address so anything a member sends back is read by a human - a hard bounce, or silence, at the moment someone writes something painful is the one failure this email cannot have.

Subject — two candidates

1. {{agent_first_name}} replied to your conversation  34 chars as “Chaim…”, 37 as “Yehoshua…”

2. {{agent_first_name}} just messaged you  23 chars, 26 with a long name  ← I’d take this one

Both name a person and neither reveals anything about the subject matter, so the anonymity test is a wash. Two things separate them. Length: the iOS lock screen cuts around 35-40 characters, so option 1 fits for “Chaim” but a longer agent name pushes it over - “Yehoshua replied to your conver…” - while option 2 fits whatever the name is. Tone: “just messaged you” is immediate and lands like a person, not a system notice, which is what gets it opened. It also covers the case where an agent writes first rather than replying, so one subject line works for both.

Two caveats on option 2, both easy to live with. The word just is only true if the email goes out immediately - if sends are ever queued or batched, drop it: {{agent_first_name}} messaged you, 18 characters, keeps everything else. And “X just messaged you” is the LinkedIn / Facebook notification cadence, so it reads slightly like a social alert; harmless here, but worth knowing that is the echo.

If option 2 wins, the big line inside the card should change to match it - right now the card still reads “Chaim replied to your conversation.” Not changed yet, since that is the design and you have not picked.

Preheader

{{email_preview}}

Hidden div, first element in the body. The reply’s first line only when it is a short safe greeting (Hi / Hello / Thank you, under ~60 characters), otherwise “A reply is waiting for you on GYE.” Raw reply text must never reach preview text - that is the one place an anonymity leak would land on a lock screen.

On the From / Subject mismatch - flagged Jul 28, now recommend keeping it

From says GYE Personal Support but the subject names Chaim. That is deliberate, not an oversight: it is the standard support-tool pattern (Intercom and Zendesk both do exactly this). The institutional sender governs reply expectations and inbox threading; the human name in the subject is what earns the open. The only reason to change it would be a decision to keep agents' names out of the subject entirely, and the fallback - “A reply is waiting for you” - reads like marketing and will be opened less. The agent’s name stays in the body either way: heading, byline, signature.
