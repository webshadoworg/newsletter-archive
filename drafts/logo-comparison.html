Colors unified to the GYE brand pair

Both tiles now use one purple and one yellow, so they are exact inversions of each other: #624b78 purple and #f9d772 yellow.

#624b78 is the established brand purple — it appears 70 times across GYE’s existing branded emails, and the purple tile you sent was already this color before JPEG compression nudged it to #614c77.

Rebuilt as PNG at 110×114 and displayed at 38px, so the letters are sharp on retina. The letterforms in the two files you sent were pixel-identical, so both tiles are driven from one shared mask.

Options C-E: the logo becomes the avatar

Same layout, but the tile moves out of the top-left corner and into the hero slot that held the agent's initial. No corner logo - one mark, centered.

The tradeoff: the point of the person-forward layout was that the avatar stood for a human being. With the tile there, the member sees an institution first and the agent's name second, in text. Not automatically wrong - the From name is already plain GYE - but it is a real change in what the email leads with.

Image quality: the tile is a 110×114 PNG. At 64px it renders at 1.8×, just under retina, so the letters go slightly soft on a phone. Option E sidesteps that by drawing the tile smaller. A vector or larger PNG from the designer fixes all of them, and is needed anyway before the tile ships at this size.
