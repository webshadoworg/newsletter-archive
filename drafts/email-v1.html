The pill — one call for you

The headline inside the email mirrors the subject, so a pill also reading “5 new messages from Chaim” would put his name in the top five lines four times. The pill is now just “5 new messages.”

It fixes the handoff case too: if the thread passed to a second agent, some of those five are someone else’s, and “5 new messages” stays true where “from Chaim” wouldn’t. His name is still in the subject, the avatar and the headline. Say the word and I put the full sentence back.

Send headers

From nameGYE Support 11 chars, survives Gmail’s narrow-phone truncation. Never the agent’s name — a stable sender survives handoffs and doesn’t look spoofed.
Fromhelp@gye.org — a real monitored inbox. No no-reply@ anywhere.
Reply-ToOmitted. help@ is already the reply target.
Stray replies get read and hand-carried into the support system. Manual, low volume, and nobody ever writes into a void.
Return-Pathbounces@mail.gye.org
Subject1 unread → {{agent_first_name}} wrote back to you
2+ unread → {{unread_count}} new messages from {{agent_first_name}}
PreheaderRead it whenever you’re ready. — fixed string, hidden div, first element in the body, followed by spacer characters so the client can’t scrape the reply text into the preview line instead.
Becomes a member choice once the Email privacy setting on page 2 ships.
Snippet{{message_snippet}} — first ~250 chars of the reply, cut at the last sentence end before 250, greeting included. Inside the email only.
LinksAll point at app.gye.org. Mail comes from gye.org.

From name, subject and preheader are send-time values, not template HTML. The subject is one branch on unread_count > 1 — the same branch that shows the pill.

Cartoon avatars — two notes for the illustrator

The avatar is a 64px lavender circle showing the initial. When {{agent_photo_url}} is set the cartoon fills it, so nothing has to change when the art lands.

  • Draw the circle into the PNG, on #eee8f3. Outlook ignores border-radius and would square off a rectangular image.
  • Ship at 128×128 for a 64px slot, so it’s sharp on retina.

The alt text is the agent’s initial, styled to match the circle — so with images off (Outlook’s default) it degrades to exactly the letter version above, not a broken-image box.
