The post

Im pinching myself. Is it really me?! Wrapping up Day 94?!Can this be really happening?! I thought i had long lost this battle?! I thought i was going to take this struggle w me to the grave?! What is going on here?! ( dont need to give ideas to anyone... I've basically done almost all of it...and each year was getting lower and lower, unaware to even myself how I was stooping so low)

I feel like something is finally working. Let's see, what did i do different this time around?! What gave me the strength to join with a mentor and get real day to day, week by week guidance?! I'm literally in disbelief thay I made it this far. Im not here to brag at all, im actually humbled and full aware of all these choshuve people growing with gye that have falls and keep having these solid streaks and falling again and learning more things about themselves etc.. I'm here to just make all those first time doubters aware that if you feel like there a bottomless pit and no real way to change your trajectory, I am here standing tall going into Day 95, and Soo looking fwd to stand in front of hashem this rosh hashana with such gratitude and presence of mind l, like never before in my life. Every Rosh Hashana and elul in my life has been a roller coaster of self doubt and double life trucking... holding onto some little hope that I can make it clean 30 days until rosh hashana so hashem will look at me somehow better.. but this year someway and somehow thanks to the help of this beautiful platform and of course mainly my mentor I am looking fwd to being mamlich hashem way differently. I still know I have a long way to go and much to learn about myself but I am hopeful that it will happen, not doubtful..

One other thing i want to share with all those who feel this is too much to dig out of. I vividly remenber last year yamim noraim. Even though I was my old self and hadn't yet tasted any real success (besides maybe a 30-45 day streak here and here) I was somehow able to tap into the tefillos and elevated mindset of the davening, but i was using my desperation and feeling of helplessness to fuel my davening. I recall feeling so at loss and total failure that I was somehow able to direct my state of sorrow and despair directly to hashem and honestly begging like an uni bapesach to open up for me a chudo shell machat.

This is a short snippet of my past year, and looking to wrap it up on a high bezras hashem.

Maybe this can be an inspiration for someone who is in a similar place again this year heading in to rosh hashana feeling low.
