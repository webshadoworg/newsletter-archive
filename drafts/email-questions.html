Question 2 — subject  ·  Jul 30

The subject follows the count, same branch as the pill.

  • 1 unread — “Chaim wrote back to you” — 23 chars, 26 with the longest staff name
  • 2+ unread — “5 new messages from Chaim” — 25 chars, 28 with the longest name

Mendy’s wording, and better than either of my candidates: it clears the ~35-char lock-screen cut in both states, and it does something neither of mine did — it puts the urgency in the subject line instead of only inside the email. A count buried in the body only nudges someone who already opened it. This one reaches the person who hasn’t, which is the whole point of having a count. Both states previewed on page 1.

Question 3 — preheader  ·  Jul 30

Generic for now: “Read it whenever you’re ready.”

The agent’s real first line never reaches a lock screen. Also better than the generic line I proposed (“A reply is waiting for you on GYE”), because it removes pressure instead of adding it — these are often people not answering because it already feels like too much.

“For now” has a specific end: the Email privacy radio on the preference page. Once a member has actively picked “Show message details,” the real first line is a choice they made and can go in. Until then it’s a default nobody consented to.

Question 4 — from name  ·  Jul 30

GYE Support. Frozen.

11 characters, so it survives Gmail’s narrow-phone sender truncation. “GYE Personal Support” is out — “Personal Support” reads like a helpline to anyone glancing at an inbox list. The role line under the agent’s name matches: “GYE Support team.”

Question 5 — avatars  ·  Jul 30

Cartoon avatars are coming.

Good outcome — it makes the person-forward layout the right call permanently, and a cartoon is better here than a real photo would be: warm and identifiable without a staff member’s actual face sitting in an anonymity-sensitive inbox. The slot is already built as {{agent_photo_url}} falling back to the letter, so nothing changes when the art lands. Two notes for the illustrator on page 1.

Decided earlier, not revisited

  • Send from help@gye.org, no no-reply@ anywhere visible
  • Replies land in help@gye.org, get read, and are hand-carried into the support system. Never bounced, and the email never claims replies go unseen
  • No reply notice in the email at all (Mendy, Jul 30)
  • Button: Open conversation — correct at any unread count, so no plural to match
  • Count pill hidden entirely at 1
