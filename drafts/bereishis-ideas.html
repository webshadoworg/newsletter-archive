A

עם נחש לא מדברים — you do not argue with the thought

Hebrew, GYE Israel · my rank 1 of 8

RECOMMEND for this issue

The nachash opens on Chava with a question he knows is false (Rashi). The lie was not the goal. Starting a conversation was the goal. The Ohr HaChaim then supplies the mechanism: occupying the mind with a desire even in order to argue against it strengthens the desire. So agreeing to the debate is already the loss.

Sources
Ohr HaChaim, Vayikra 18:2VERIFIED בְּנִי אִם יְפַתּוּךָ חַטָּאִים – פֵּרוּשׁ בְּחִינַת הַחֵטְא – אַל תֹּאבֶה – פֵּרוּשׁ לֹא תִּכָּנֵס עִם פִּתּוּיֶיךָ בְּטוֹעֵן וְנִטְעָן, כִּי אֲרִיכוּת הַרְגָּשַׁת הַחוֹשֵׁב אֲפִלּוּ לִבְחִינַת הַשּׁוֹלֵל תַּפְעִיל הַגְבָּרַת הַחֵפֶץ וְתַהֲפֹךְ הָרָצוֹן אֵלֶיהָ — word for word, pulled from Sefaria.

Rav Gifter on Chava — the diamond or the scratch

Living Appreciation 241 · my rank 2 of 8

RECOMMEND as a short closer, if the source checks out

Adam names his wife חַוָּה, mother of all living, after the cheit, after childbirth was made painful and death decreed. Rav Gifter: he chose not to look at her error but at the virtue, that she and her daughters would bear that pain to bring life. Every Jew is a diamond. Do you look at the diamond or at the scratch?

C

אני בכלל לא חסר אונים — what Step 1 actually says

Hebrew, GYE Israel · my rank 3 of 8

RECOMMEND holding for a future issue, not this one

“Powerless” was translated into Hebrew as חסר אונים, which carries a flavour of hapless, clueless, a nebach. So members refuse the word. But אונים means כוח, strength, nothing more. Powerless does not mean I do not know what to do. It means I do not have the strength to do it. Toothache and a broken car: I am not stupid, I am not a dentist. And as long as I deny that, I cannot turn to the One who does have the strength. Closes on אנחנו חסרי אונים מול התאווה, אבל לא מול פעולות ההחלמה.

Sources
Bereishis 49:3VERIFIED רְאוּבֵן בְּכֹרִי אַתָּה כֹּחִי וְרֵאשִׁית אוֹנִי — exact.
Yeshayah 40:29VERIFIED נֹתֵן לַיָּעֵף כֹּחַ וּלְאֵין אוֹנִים עׇצְמָה יַרְבֶּה — exact.
Yeshayah 40:26UNVERIFIED The Hebrew has רוֹב אוֹנִים וְאַמִּיץ כֹּחַ. The pasuk reads מֵרֹב אוֹנִים וְאַמִּיץ כֹּחַ. One letter, worth fixing in the Hebrew too.
Against the archive

NEVER RUN “Powerless” appears in the archive only inside member posts, never as a teaching. The word-study has never run in English.

Why it is worth it

The best writing in either drop. It takes the single most misunderstood word in the programme and settles it in two meshalim.

What is wrong with it

It is a 12-step column, not a Bereishis dvar Torah, and the issue already has its four sections. The pasuk it hangs on is Vayechi, not Bereishis, so nothing is lost by waiting.

Bishvil bikurim — gratitude as the purpose of creation

Living Appreciation 241 · my rank 5 of 8

Available, but it is not this issue’s thread

Read בראשית as בשביל ראשית, for the sake of the first fruits. The Alshich: the whole point of bikurim is hakaras hatov. So gratitude is the reason the world was made. Then the Ohr HaChaim on why only man gets טוב מאוד — good is only recognisable through someone who can recognise it.

Sources
“Bereishis Rabbah 1:6”WRONG CITE Wrong on two counts. The maamar is at Bereishis Rabbah 1:4, not 1:6, and it does not say bikurim alone: בזכות שלשה דברים נברא העולם, בזכות חלה, ובזכות מעשרות, ובזכות בכורים. Challah and maaser come first. Taking bikurim out on its own to make the gratitude point is the compiler’s move, not the midrash’s.
Ohr HaChaim “(31:31)”VERIFIED Typo for 1:31. The content is right: כי אין הבחנת טובה ניכרת אלא באמצעות האדם, כי אליו יוכר ויובחן הטוב ויודה למטיב.
Alshich, Ki Savo 26:2UNVERIFIED Not on Sefaria. Not checked.

NEVER RUN No hits.

Solid Bereishis material and the Ohr HaChaim is lovely.

F

Meshech Chochmah — eating the permitted fruit was itself a mitzvah

Living Appreciation 241 · my rank 6 of 8

RECOMMEND against for GYE

מכל עץ הגן אכל תאכל was not permission, it was a command. The Yerushalmi that a person will answer for the permitted pleasures he refused. And the kicker: had Adam told Chava that eating the garden’s fruit was a mitzvah, keeping that mitzvah could have protected her from the forbidden one.

Sources
Yerushalmi Kiddushin 4:12UNVERIFIED Standard citation in the literature, but Sefaria’s segmentation of that halachah does not contain the line and its phrase search returns nothing usable. Not confirmed.
Meshech Chochmah, Bereishis 2:16UNVERIFIED Not on Sefaria. Not checked.
Against the archive

NEVER RUN No hits.

What is wrong with it

The framing. “Enjoying the permitted thing protects you from the forbidden thing” is true in its place, but a reader of this newsletter can hear it as a licence, and a few of them will hear it that way on purpose. I would not put it in front of this audience without a lot of hedging, and the hedging would cost more than the piece is worth.
