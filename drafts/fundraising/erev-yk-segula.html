With just a few short hours left until Yom Kippur, I want to share a powerful segula that can open the gates of Heaven for your tefillos.

The Sanz-Klausenburg Rebbe zt"l writes in Shefa Chaim that without a kohen and Beis Hamikdash to bring us atonement, “Our only strength is our voice: to pray and cry out to Hashem.” He urges us to address the spiritual barriers, often related to Tikun Habris, that can prevent our tefillos from being fully accepted. (Moadim, Derush 19.)

What can we do about those barriers before Yom Kippur?

The Ateres Yehoshua teaches that a great tikkun is helping others strengthen their kedushah, and that Erev Yom Kippur is an especially meaningful day for doing this. Here’s why:

1. The Shomer Emunim teaches that when a person resists a single temptation related to kedushah, it is an especially auspicious moment for his tefillos to be accepted. By supporting GYE, you’re helping thousands of Jews combat countless kedushah-related nisyonos. Can you imagine how that zechus can affect your tefillos on Yom Kippur?

2. The Shem Mishmuel writes that failings in kedushah disrupt the channels of parnassah. Therefore, it is vital to remove these barriers before Yom Kippur, when we are written in the book of parnassah and life.

By supporting our Aseres Yemei Teshuva campaign, you have a rare opportunity to gain this special zechus and stand on Yom Kippur with the merit of helping thousands of Jews around the world guard their eyes and strengthen their kedushah.
