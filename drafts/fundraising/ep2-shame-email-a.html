CHAPTER TWO

THE ENEMY GOT SMARTER.

“Eventually, I learned…

I didn’t need to scare anyone.

Shame worked.

Isolation worked.

Hopelessness worked.”

He is right, and that is the uncomfortable part. Those three things work better than any website ever did. A man who is ashamed does not ask. A man who is alone believes he is the only one. A man with no hope stops trying.

So here are three of our members on those three things. None of them has seen the film.
 

You have helped me to take away the shame and normalize the struggle. You have helped me to no longer see my struggle as an overwhelming crisis, but rather as a difficult but manageable issue

On the shame.
 

For all this time i was walking around with the mindset that there are normal people and then the losers like me. To find this site with close to 25,000 members and so much acceptance, chizuk, advice etc etc... was a remarkable breath of fresh air. It is because of you - yes you - who are there for me that i have now bh passed the 30 day mark despite very challenging circumstances.

On the isolation.
 

I was depressed before I found gye, I wud cry regularly, thinking I was the only person in the world doing these things. I thought I was living a lie, learning the whole day and also doing the worst of aveiros when no one was looking. I wud sometimes lay in my bed wondering when Hashem was gonna kill me. Today I reached Day 48.

On the hopelessness.

Not one of them describes a program. What each of them describes is the moment somebody on the other end was not shocked.

You can hear what one of those conversations sounds like. Thirty seconds, no names.
