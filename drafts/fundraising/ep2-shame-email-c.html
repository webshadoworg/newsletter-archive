Years of struggle, then a few weeks. The answer is simpler than you would think.

Dear Friend of GYE,

Here is a fair question to ask before you give us a dollar. A man struggles with this for years, in some cases decades. He finds GYE. A few weeks later he is somewhere he never reached on his own. Not everyone, and not always quickly. But often enough that it needs explaining.

It is not that we are clever.

What held him still all those years was not the pull. It was two things he believed on the way in: that he is the only one like this, and that he cannot change. Believe both of those and hiding is the only sensible plan left. A man not living up to his own standards, certain he never will, who concludes that he must simply be damaged.

Now notice something. Both of those beliefs are false.

Which means they are not feelings to be soothed. They are facts he has wrong.

So the first thing GYE hands a man is not a filter and not a plan. It is two pieces of information.

Other people are exactly like you.

Many of them have already succeeded.
 

For all this time i was walking around with the mindset that there are normal people and then the losers like me. To find this site with close to 25,000 members and so much acceptance, chizuk, advice etc etc... was a remarkable breath of fresh air. It is because of you - yes you - who are there for me that i have now bh passed the 30 day mark despite very challenging circumstances.

Nothing in him had to be rebuilt. He was carrying a false description of himself, and somebody finally corrected it. That is why it can happen fast.

Hope is not a mood we talk a man into. It is what is left once the facts are straight, and it unlocks kochos he did not know were there.
