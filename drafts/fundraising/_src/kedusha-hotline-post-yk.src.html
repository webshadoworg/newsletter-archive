Did you see Mishpacha's article on Project Kedusha, GYE's latest project?

Here's the backstory. Not everyone who comes to GYE is fighting an addictive habit; many just need the right guidance on how to protect their devices. But between dozens of kosher phones, filters, kosher AI tools, and educational resources, few people have the time or knowledge to figure out what's right for them.

So we wondered, what if we brought every organization working on kedusha under one roof, and we made it easy for everyone to see what’s the best fit for them?

That idea became Project Kedusha, bringing close to 200 vetted Kedusha resources into one place, and it comes to life in three ways:

  1. The Kedusha Guide, in print.
  2. Kedusha.com - ask our AI chat a question and get instant guidance.
  3. 1-866-KEDUSHA (533-8742) - talk through any Kedusha dilemma with our latest AI voice technology. No internet needed. (Not to be confused with the new GYE hotline that we told you about before Yom Kippur at 1-877-GYE-4-YOU (877-493-4968).)

The hotline will blow your mind. Ask it anything, like “my daughter wants to upgrade her phone for her bas mitzvah,” and see what comes back. Free, anonymous, and always on.

Rav Yosef Elefant of the Mir called Project Kedusha “a game changer”: instead of helping only those who struggle, GYE can now upgrade the kedusha of all of Klal Yisrael.
