Something new, as of this week. GYE has a hotline you can call and just talk.

Imagine a GYE mentor with endless patience, awake at any hour of the day or night. That is close to what this is.

It is not a person. It is AI, trained on GYE’s own materials and our 3-Pillar approach, and it holds a real conversation with you out loud.

GYE has run hotlines for years. But there is nothing like a conversation. It is the most natural thing in the world: pick up the phone, dial the number, talk.

What it is good for:

  • Building your plan, out loud, instead of on paper.
  • A boost when the motivation has gone out of it.
  • After a fall: what happened, why, and what to do now.
  • Thinking through anything you are stuck on, in the car or on the way somewhere.

It is completely anonymous. Nothing to log into, no name to give, and nobody on the other end who could recognise you, because there is nobody on the other end. If you want to be extra careful, call from a blocked number.

You do not need internet for it either. Any phone will do.

It is brand new and still in beta, so use your own judgment: it does not replace a real mentor. And if you want a real person, leave a message and one of us will call you back.
