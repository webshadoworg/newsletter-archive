The Yetzer Hara's whole strategy here is shame. Make a man so embarrassed to say it to another human being that he keeps it to himself. Alone with the secret, he decides he is hopelessly messed up.

On a first call we do the opposite, and you can hear it happen. In one conversation, a weight comes off his shoulders. And once he believes he can change, he starts to change.

Here is what one member wrote afterward:

“Before I found GYE, I wud cry regularly, thinking I was the only person in the world doing these things. I thought I was living a lie, learning the whole day and also doing the worst of aveiros when no one was looking. I wud sometimes lay in my bed wondering when Hashem was gonna kill me.

“Today I reached Day 48. Thank you from the bottom of my heart. Thank you for saving my neshama. Thank you for giving me life.”

— a 21-year-old member
