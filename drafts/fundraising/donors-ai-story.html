What AI did this year

By the time they are signed up we already know why they came and what their goal is. And when someone does not finish, you read those conversations to learn what is holding people back and how to improve.
  2. Bots for the main GYE tools, by conversation instead of the hard way. Talk it through and it guides you to a robust plan, what to do next, where you are stuck. You monitor the conversations, quality is high, all trained by GYE, continuously improved.

  4. AI on the programming team, so you do more in less time. Your phrase: these new superpowers. A wide variety of new projects and solutions came out of that this year. Kedusha.com is one example.

Your thesis: AI is not replacing human support, it is complementing it.
