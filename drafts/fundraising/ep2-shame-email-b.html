A letter we were sent. We are forwarding the part of it that is ours to forward.

Dear Friend of GYE,

We were going to write to you about Chapter Two, which is out now, and in which our narrator explains that he stopped needing to frighten anybody once he worked out that shame does the job for him.

Instead we are going to forward you a letter.

It came from a member. We have cut the middle of it, where he asks us to add a motzei Shabbos email and tells us that one of our own techniques still confuses him. The rest is his, spelling and all.
 

You guys are the absolute best people in the world, I really don’t think there are words to relate just how much you changed my life. The most impactful part of the email is the fact that it’s daily. That daily reminder every morning to keep an eye on my kedusha. It also works in some kind of spiritual way, somehow just by spending a few minutes every morning on kedusha, my whole day I have less nisyonos. … I was depressed before I found gye, I wud cry regularly, thinking I was the only person in the world doing these things. I thought I was living a lie, learning the whole day and also doing the worst of aveiros when no one was looking. I wud sometimes lay in my bed wondering when Hashem was gonna kill me. Today I reached Day 48. Thank you from the bottom of my heart. Thank you for saving my neshama. Thank you for giving me life.

He was wrong about being the only person in the world doing these things, and nobody had ever told him so. That is the whole of it. He did not need a program, he needed one person to not be shocked.

Another member put the same thing in the film’s own vocabulary, which he has never read:
 

You have helped me to take away the shame and normalize the struggle. You have helped me to no longer see my struggle as an overwhelming crisis, but rather as a difficult but manageable issue
