P.S. We were going to tell you what your donation makes possible. Then we realized you could just call it.

A few days ago, we launched something new:

1-877-GYE-4-YOU

877-493-4968

It’s a free, AI-powered phone service you can talk to at any hour, from any phone.

Because the hard moments don’t keep office hours. They come at two in the morning, sitting in the car, or ten minutes before you have to walk into a room and act like everything is fine.

And sometimes, you don’t feel ready to call a GYE friend or mentor, or write a post on the forum. You just need somewhere to start.

Now you can pick up the phone and say what’s on your mind.

An AI voice responds, asks questions, and helps you think through your next step, drawing on GYE’s own materials and our 3-Pillar approach.

If you remember our old hotlines, you remember the menus. Press 1. Press 4. Try to remember where that recording was.

This one has no menus. You simply talk, and it talks back.

You can use it to work out a practical plan, get some perspective when your motivation has faded, or think through what happened after a fall and what to do next. Sometimes, it helps just to say out loud what’s been going around in your head.

No app. No internet. No smartphone. No account to create and no name to give.

It does have limits. It’s AI, and it can misunderstand you or give an unhelpful answer. It won’t check on you tomorrow or provide the accountability of a real relationship. A mentor or GYE friend still matters.

The service is also brand-new. If an answer misses the mark, please tell us so we can improve it. If you’d like to speak with a real person from GYE, leave a message at the end of the call and one of us will get back to you. For immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Give it four minutes tonight.

Last year’s campaign helped make this possible. Your gift this year helps keep it free and helps us build what comes next.
