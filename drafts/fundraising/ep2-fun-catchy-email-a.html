CHAPTER TWO

THE ENEMY GOT SMARTER.
Watch Chapter Two

He opens pleased with himself.

So I went everywhere. Into their pockets. Into every quiet moment.

Then he takes off the dark glasses, makes himself a coffee, and explains that the costume was never the point.

Eventually, I learned... I didn’t need to scare anyone. Shame worked. Isolation worked. Hopelessness worked.

Three tools, and not one of them is a screen. Which is the whole reason a filter was never going to be enough, and why your giving has never gone into blocking software.

Fifteen seconds in, he opens a folder, looks up, and the smile goes.

So they connected people. Removed the shame. Studied why people fall... and how they rise.

That is a list of things you bought. Isolation lost to a forum and a partner on the other end of a phone. Shame lost to a hotline a man can call at 1-877-GYE-4-YOU at two in the morning without giving his name. Hopelessness lost to twenty years of watching what actually works and then handing it to the next man for free.

He closes the folder and walks to the window.

Every time I got smarter... so did they.

We are not going to pretend that is not the nicest thing anyone said about us all year.

And then, sipping his coffee, he says the one thing in the film that is aimed at you and not at us.

For almost twenty years... they’ve kept building. And you make that possible. Please don’t.

He knows exactly who keeps him up at night, and it is not us.

That is his ask. Ours is the opposite one, and there are only a few days left to answer it.
