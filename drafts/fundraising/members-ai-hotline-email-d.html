1-877-GYE-4-YOU

It’s a free, AI-powered phone service you can talk to at any hour, from any phone.

You may already have a mentor you speak with, friends you check in with, or a thread on the forum. But there are moments in between those conversations when you could use some help thinking things through.

It’s late. Something caught you off guard. You’re losing motivation, or you’re trying to remember how to put one of the tools into practice.

Now you can pick up the phone and say what’s on your mind.

An AI voice responds, asks questions, and helps you think through your next step, drawing on GYE’s own materials and our 3-Pillar approach.

If you remember our old hotlines, you remember the menus. Press 1. Press 4. Try to remember where that recording was.

This one has no menus. You simply talk, and it talks back.

You can use it to work out a practical plan, get some perspective when your motivation has faded, or think through what happened after a fall and what to do next. Sometimes, it helps just to say out loud what’s been going around in your head.

You can learn through it too. Revisit something from a mini-course, ask about a concept you didn’t quite understand, or explore how a tool applies to the situation you’re facing today.

No app. No internet. No smartphone needed. No account to create and no name to give.

And if you’ve spoken with a GYE mentor, you already know how much the person on the other end matters. Someone who knows your story, understands your matzav, and cares how you’re doing. AI can be useful, but it can’t give you that relationship.

Think of this as another tool to use alongside the support you already have. If you’d like to speak with someone from GYE, you can leave a message at the end of the call and we’ll get back to you.

The AI can also misunderstand or get things wrong. If an answer misses the mark, please let us know so we can improve it. It isn’t an emergency service. If you’re in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Give it four minutes tonight.
