The handle of the axe comes from the forest itself.

Dear Friend of GYE,

Right now, somewhere, there is a Yid sitting in the dark holding a phone that cannot open a browser. It is two in the morning. He is losing, and he knows exactly what he is about to do.

He can dial 1-877-GYE-4-YOU and, within seconds, be talking to something that understands his situation, works through it with him, and can put him through to a human being if that is what he needs. No waiting. No queue. No appointment for Tuesday.

A year ago we could not do that. You paid for it.

While AI is creating all sorts of challenges, we’re finding it incredible as a tool to assist in the fight, as the talmud says מיניה וביה אבא ניזול ביה נרגא. The very thing that made this fight harder than it has ever been is now the thing carrying help to a man at two in the morning.

Let me be clear about what this is not. We are not handing this work over to a machine, and we never will. Nothing we built this year cost one human being his job here. A man gets out of this because another person knows his name, expects to hear from him and notices when he goes quiet. That is what makes someone committed and what keeps him consistent, and no bot will ever come close.

So this year your money went into both sides at once. On the human side, full-time support staff, a large team of volunteer mentors, a mentor coordinator so that nobody falls between the cracks, and a mentor training program we are launching now, the women’s pilot first, so the mentors themselves keep getting better at it.

What AI covers is the hours a human being cannot. Nobody can be awake for every member at every moment. And a weak moment does not always need a person in the room. Very often it needs the same process, the questions, the plan, the honest look at what just happened, and that can now happen at two in the morning with nobody else awake. Our approach stands on three pillars: motivation, planning and connection. Connection is a person, permanently. The other two no longer have to wait for office hours.

So the hotline now holds thirty calls at the same moment and the chat on our site has no limit at all, and nobody is ever told to come back later. Signing up is a conversation instead of a form. Every GYE tool is something a man can simply talk through until he walks away with a real plan, which is work that most people never got around to when it was courses and worksheets. Members who have been with us for years tell us it opened up ways of thinking they had never reached.

We are pioneers in this field, and we are not leaving one stone unturned, because Klal Yisrael should not be getting the best a small organization can manage. It should be getting the very best solutions that exist, so that a man’s journey out is as smooth and as fast as it can possibly be. Next year that means teenagers, parents and spouses, each with a platform built for them instead of a version of somebody else’s.

But nothing here answers the phone at two in the morning on its own. It runs on people who decide to pay for a stranger they will never meet, before Yom Kippur, while there is still time to be the reason he had somebody to call.

$180 carries one member for a full year. Under fifty cents a day.
