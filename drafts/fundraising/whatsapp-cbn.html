Five things that matter when posting

  • The link goes on the last line, on its own, with nothing after it. No full stop, no emoji, no extra words. WhatsApp builds its preview from the first link it finds and a trailing character can break the link itself.
  • Do not swap the links between placements. Group posts use link 1, the two status posts use links 2 and 3. That is the only way to tell afterwards what actually worked.
  • No emoji added to the text. It reads as an advert in a forward, and the villain framing already does the work in words.
  • Do not add bold or italic markers. The lines are short on purpose and the first line is the hook.
  • Every link lands on the same page, charidy.com/gye, which carries the films and the donate button. So the text can say watch and donate without sending anybody two places.
