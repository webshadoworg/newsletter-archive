What GYE is doing with AI, and what we will never hand to a machine.

Dear Friend of GYE,

While AI is creating all sorts of challenges, we’re finding it incredible as a tool to assist in the fight, as the talmud says מיניה וביה אבא ניזיל ביה נרגא.

So we did not watch this from the sidelines. GYE is building on the very latest AI models and practices there are, and we are not leaving one stone unturned. Here is what that means, in plain terms.

What we will never hand to a machine

Our approach stands on three pillars: motivation, planning and connection. Connection means a human being, and no bot comes close. What a man says out loud to another person commits him in a way nothing else does. Judgment calls and accountability stay human. So this year we paid to make that side stronger.

  • Two full-time support staff on the phones and the messages.
  • A case management system, so nobody falls between the cracks.
  • We are launching a mentor training program. The women’s pilot is releasing now, the men’s follows. The training itself runs on AI: a mentor rehearses a hard conversation, then gets tested on how he handled it.

What AI added

It never sleeps and it never has a queue. Our hotline can take thirty calls at the same moment. The chat on our site has no limit at all. Two in the morning, no appointment, nobody ahead of you.

It is also far more engaging than courses, worksheets and exercises ever were. Members who have been with GYE for years tell us the bots opened up ways of thinking they had never reached, and gave their journey a real push. It turns out to be very good at the work a person cannot do alone: understanding himself, thinking out loud, taking a problem apart.

Three things we built with it this year:

  1. Signing up is a conversation, not a form. A new member is interviewed, answered and understood before he is even registered.
  2. The GYE tools work by talking. Building a real plan used to be work most people never got around to. Now a member talks it through and comes out with one.
  3. The hotline, which is the big one. A Yid with no internet at all can call 1-877-GYE-4-YOU and get real help on the spot, and ask to be put through to a human being whenever he wants one.

They are safe because we built them that way. Trained on everything GYE has learned in twenty years, with our own safeguards, reviewed by our own team, and improved every month.

What it makes possible next

GYE has always served the struggling adult well. In the coming year we are building dedicated platforms for teenagers, parents and spouses, so that whoever arrives at our door finds something built for his situation.

We are pioneers in this field and we intend to stay there, because Klal Yisrael should have the very best solutions that exist, not the best a small organization can manage. That is what makes a man’s journey out as smooth and as fast as it can be. And we can only do it with your help.
