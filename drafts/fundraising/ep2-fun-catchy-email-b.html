Dear Friend of GYE,

The following complaint reached our office. We are passing it on unedited.

To whom it may concern,

I have held this position since the beginning of time and I have never once had to put anything in writing.

For the record, my system was excellent. So I went everywhere. Into their pockets. Into every quiet moment. And I did it without ever raising my voice, because eventually, I learned... I didn’t need to scare anyone. Shame worked. Isolation worked. Hopelessness worked.

Three tools. No overhead. Best century I ever had.

Then a small operation in Brooklyn took an interest in my methods. So they connected people. Removed the shame. Studied why people fall... and how they rise.

I would like to be precise about the damage. A man who used to sit alone at two in the morning with nowhere to go now has a number to dial, 1-877-GYE-4-YOU, and he does not even have to give his name. He is answered in seconds, at any hour, and nobody is waiting ahead of him. Do you understand what that does to shame? It needs a locked door. They took the door off.

I adapted, naturally. I always adapt. Every time I got smarter... so did they.

So I reviewed the file, and I have located the real problem. It is not the organization. Organizations run out of money. The problem is that somebody keeps paying for this one.

That somebody is you.

For almost twenty years... they’ve kept building. And you make that possible.

Please don’t.

Sincerely,
Your Yetzer Hara

He is right about the second to last line and wrong about the last one, and you are the only person who can settle which.
