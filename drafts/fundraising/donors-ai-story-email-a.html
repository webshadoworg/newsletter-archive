Then artificial intelligence grew up.

Dear Friend of GYE,

Last year, we thought we had reached the ceiling. We had built everything the technology of the day allowed us to build.

Then, in the months since our last campaign, artificial intelligence grew up.

We are convinced the Ribono Shel Olam put this tool in our hands for a reason. It lets a small organization reach a struggling Yid with a depth that an organization our size has no business having. So here is an honest account of what we did with it this year.

First, the thing we want to say plainly. AI is not replacing human support at GYE. Nothing replaces a human being who picks up the phone. AI makes that human help go further, and this year we put money and effort into both sides.

The human side got stronger

  • We built a case management system, so that no member falls between the cracks. Every member gets followed up with, instead of being left to find his own way.
  • We launched a mentor training program, so our volunteer mentors keep sharpening their skills. The women’s program is live now. The men’s follows in the coming months.
  • We now have two full-time support staff answering the phones and the messages.

What we built with AI

  1. Signing up is a conversation now, not a form. Someone arriving at GYE is guided through a real interview. He gets answers and feedback on the spot, and by the time he is registered we already understand why he came and what he is hoping for. And when someone begins and does not finish, we read that conversation too, because it tells us what is stopping people from getting help at all.
  2. The GYE tools can now be used by talking. Building a real plan used to be work that most people never got around to. Now a member talks it through, figures out where he is stuck and what to do next. Every one of these is trained on the GYE approach, the conversations are reviewed by our own team, and they keep getting better.
  3. The hotline, which is the big one. A Yid with no internet at all can pick up a phone, call 1-877-GYE-4-YOU, and get real help at two in the morning. And if he wants a human being, he can be put through to one, or leave a message and we will call him back.

There is a quieter fourth item. AI has made our own development team far faster, so we build more in less time. That is the reason this year produced more new projects than any year before it. We’re able to develop solutions that we never dreamed was possible. Kedusha.com is one of them.

And what it makes possible next

For most of twenty years, GYE has served one kind of Yid well: the struggling adult. In the coming year we are building dedicated platforms for teenagers, parents and spouses, so that whoever arrives at our door finds something built for their situation rather than a version of somebody else’s.

None of it runs on the technology. It runs on you.
