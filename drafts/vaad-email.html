I wanted to let you know that we’re opening new Vaad groups right after Sukkos, and I’d like to invite you to join one.

The Vaad is a small group that meets once a week, going through the GYE material together. Every group is led by a veteran GYE member who’s been where you are and volunteers his time to run it. The groups cover three areas: plans and motivation, tools and perspectives, and connection and life balance.

Guys who join usually find it’s the piece that makes everything else stick. Meeting every week keeps you consistent, and the conversations tend to show you things about yourself you wouldn’t have noticed on your own. It’s also a good time, which people don’t expect going in.

It’s fully anonymous and there’s no cost.
