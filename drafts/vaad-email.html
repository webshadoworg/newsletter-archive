Additional Vaad Groups Starting Soon

We would like to offer you something that could change the year ahead: a place in one of our new Vaad Support Groups.

We’re opening additional Vaad groups after Sukkos. Each group is a small chevra that meets weekly, people who understand exactly what you’re carrying, working through tools of GYE together. It’s a steady anchor to check in, connect, and keep moving forward, and you don’t have to do any of it alone.

Every group runs three tracks:

—  Plans and Motivation
—  Tools and Perspectives
—  Connection and Life Balance

Think of it as one of the most powerful tools we have. All you have to do is sign up, and be ready to start right after Yom Tov.
