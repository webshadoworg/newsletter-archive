You were right — I judged the book on the wrong chapter

Aug 16 I read the free 15-page sample and told you the book was off-theme for the series. You said: "the main chapter I was referring to are different and better." You were right. Here's what changed.

What I got wrong

The free ArtScroll sample is pp. 206–220, "Building Muscle" — *nisyonos are a gift, the harder the test the greater you become.* That chapter really is wrong for our reader, and I stand by that part. But it's the weakest chapter in the book for us, and I let it stand in for all 488 pages.

Chapter 7, "Essence or Circumstance" (pp. 121–135) is the one you meant. It is written to a man who is still falling, and it says so out loud.

The three answers you asked for

1. Is there a 5-step program? No — not anywhere in the book. Chayim may be thinking of a different Fishoff resource; worth asking him. But Section Five is a real ladder: six Concepts in a deliberate order, pp. 373–466.

2. Is the good chapter on-theme for a man who hasn't stopped yet? Yes. Chapter 7, p128:

"Authentic Yiddishkeit clearly believes that even after sinning over and over, day after day, year after year, all the receptors that connect us to Hashem are still completely intact and ready to be used! They don't become musty, dusty, or rusty!"

GPS! p128 — under the subhead "Even after so many sins, your essence is still intact"

3. Should it go into the series? Your call, and now it's a real question — on Aug 16 I said no, but the reason I gave (off-theme) was wrong about the book as a whole. The four parts are still locked from Aug 10, and you were clear then: *less is more.*

What I'd actually take from it

The book is *"inspired by the writings of the Nesivos Shalom"* — its own words on the title page, not "translation." So by your Aug 10 rule it's a pointer, not a source: use it as an index and cite the Nesivos Shalom. That also sidesteps the copyright question entirely.

The single highest-value locus in the whole book, and it's the series thesis in the Nesivos Shalom's own words — נתיבות שלום חלק ב׳ עמוד שמ״ט:

"just as there is a way to connect to Hashem from a spirit of greatness, so too there is a way to connect to Hashem specifically when you are in a low and dark situation."

Nesivos Shalom II p. 349, quoted in GPS! Concept Six, p462

Second, נתיבות שלום עניני פורים … הסתר פנים אות ד׳ — yesod hasimcha: *"to believe that inside all the concealment the Holy One is with him — I am Hashem who dwells with them even inside their tumah."*

Neither of those needs GPS at all once we have the locus. That's the whole value of the book in one line.

Three practical notes

  • Buy a copy. ~$25. GYE should own it, and a legitimate copy is what any reprint request rests on.
  • Reprinting a passage needs ArtScroll's written permission — their copyright page says the rights

*will be strictly enforced.* They grant it routinely (the free sample is footer-stamped "reproduced with permission"), so ask; don't rely on a credit line.

  • Verify every citation. The book misprints its own — I checked one at 18×: p209 cites

*אשרי הגבר אשר תיסרנו י-ה* as תהלים מד:יב, and it's צד:יב (94:12). That's a misprint in the printed book, not a scanning artifact.

One thing about the scan itself

Your PDF was merged from three runs and two of them are in reverse page order — so anything reading it front-to-back reads the back half of the book backwards. That's why the first summary of Section Five had four of the six Concepts mislabelled. It's fixed on my end (the pages are now indexed by their printed folio), and nothing was lost. Just don't cite a page number off the raw PDF.
