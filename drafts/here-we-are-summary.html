Your Tanya, checked

Verbatim, word for word

I ran your Hebrew against Sefaria’s Tanya, Part I, Likkutei Amarim 28. It matches the printed text exactly, with nothing dropped or joined. Your English is a fair loose rendering of it. Nothing here needs fixing. The three notes below are things standing next to your quote that the column could use, not corrections to it.

The sentence before your quote is the reason

Your excerpt begins "when the Divine soul pushes itself". The clause immediately before it is the one that explains why: ונודע דרך הנלחמים וכן הנאבקים יחד, כשאחד מתגבר אזי השני מתאמץ להתגבר גם כן בכל מאמצי כחו. The way of fighters and wrestlers is that when one starts winning, the other strains harder. That is the whole answer to "why davening, and why worse than any other time", and it is one sentence.

The chapter opens with the practical ruling

Before any of the explanation, ch. 28 says what to do: do not let your heart dwell on the thought, turn your mind from it at once, and do not be the fool who tries to elevate it, because that was said for tzaddikim, whose stray thoughts are not their own. Then the ikar: do not be עצב נבזה over it during davening, which has to be done with great joy. אדרבה, draw fresh strength and push harder, with more joy, not less.

And the mashal right after it is a technique

A man davening with kavana while a rasha stands opposite him chattering to put him off. The answer is not to argue, not to answer him good or bad, but to act deaf and not hear. אל תען כסיל באולתו. That is BenHashemBH's silver strand, three hundred years earlier, and the two belong on the same page.
