The one thing we're missing: voices

_Sharpened Aug 27, 2026 after reading the TAG spread (`sources/tag-mishpacha-spread.pdf`). Both
Mishpacha "Introduces" spreads we have are carried by named people talking, not by descriptions of the
organization. The TAG piece says so in as many words: "We can talk about the innovations in filtering…
But mostly we should focus on the 'regular' people in towns across the world." Our draft currently has
no human voice in it at all._

What would help most, in order:

1. Three or four short quotes from named people in different places. Not one long interview. The
TAG format is a name, a city, and one sentence in their own words. Candidates: whoever runs a TAG
office that has started handing the Guide out, a rebbi or menahel who uses it, a Rav who ran the
Kedusha Challenge in his kehilla, someone at Mishor. One line each is enough.
Note the register in both spreads: everybody deflects credit. *"Its not me, its zchus harobbim,
and I am privileged."* Nobody claims anything. Please don't polish the quotes into marketing.
2. One real scene to replace my reconstructed kitchen:

  • Where were you when Project Kedusha stopped being an idea? What was the conversation?
  • What happened when the first printed copy arrived? Who did you hand it to, and what did they say?
  • One Kedusha.com conversation that surprised you, anonymized. A single real exchange is worth three

paragraphs of description.

  • Did anyone say no? Gathering other organizations' resources into one volume can't have been

simple. A moment of friction, even one that ended well, is what makes an article read as true.
3. Do you want to be quoted by name? The two pull-quotes in your name are currently my wording and
are flagged yellow on the page. They need your real words or they come out.
