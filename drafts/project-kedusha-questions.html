Questions for Yaakov

To turn the draft into a story. Last year's spread worked because one person was talking the whole way through, and we currently have no voice like that. What we need most is a scene and a few real numbers; everything else is bonus.

The one thing we're missing: a scene

  • Where were you when Project Kedusha stopped being an idea? What was the conversation, and what made it obvious?
  • What happened when the first printed copy arrived? Who did you hand it to, and what did they say?
  • One Kedusha.com conversation that surprised you, anonymized, in whatever detail you can share. A single real exchange is worth three paragraphs of description.
  • Did anyone say no? Gathering other organizations' resources into one volume can't have been simple. A moment of friction, even one that ended well, is what makes an article read as true.

Numbers we can print

  • Copies printed, and copies out the door so far.
  • How many resources, filters, devices, offices are actually listed in the Guide.
  • Kedusha.com conversations to date. Members and mentors, current.
  • Project STEP: filters installed, and how many were sponsored.
  • Kedusha Challenge and Esa Einai: this year's numbers, and whether we can name a shul or a Rav.

Attribution and accuracy

  • Rav Yosef Elefent's exact words, and his correct title. Any other endorsement we may name in print?
  • Do you want to be quoted by name, or should the piece stay in GYE's voice? Last year's source was anonymous ("Shmuel*, Mentor Coordinator"). We have two draft pull-quotes in your name that are my wording. They need your real words or they come out.
  • Mishor: who they are, why them, and what they actually do week to week.
  • 866-KEDUSHA: who picks up, a person or the AI by voice? What hours?
  • "Close to 20 years": last year's piece said 18. Is 2026 the twentieth?

The ask

  • What do you want a reader who has *no* problem to do after reading this? Buy the Guide, bookmark the site, sponsor copies, or just know it exists? The article should end on one action, not four.
  • The annual campaign: dates, goal, and whether there's a match.
  • Anything we must not say.
