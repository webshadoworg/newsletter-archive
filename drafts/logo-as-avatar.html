What changed

The GYE tile has moved out of the top-left corner and into the hero slot that used to hold the agent's initial. There is no corner logo any more - one mark, centered, at the top of the email. Three treatments below.

The tradeoff to decide: the point of the person-forward layout was that the avatar stood for a human being. With the tile there, the first thing the member sees is an institution and the person's name comes second, in text. That is not automatically wrong - the From name is already plain GYE, so this is arguably more honest about who is writing - but it is a real change in what the email leads with.

Image quality: the tile is a 110×114 PNG. At 64px it renders at 1.8×, just under retina, so the letters will be a touch soft on a phone. Option 3 sidesteps this by showing the tile smaller inside a ring. A vector or larger PNG from the designer fixes it for all three, and is needed anyway before the tile is used at this size in production.
