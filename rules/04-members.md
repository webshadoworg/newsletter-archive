# 04 Members

Printing a forum member's words in a mass email.

## Screening the handle

- **Never quote a handle that reads as a woman's name.** Two have been pulled for this. Attribute as "one member" / "another member" **and drop the hyperlink**, because the link
  itself exposes the username.
- Screen every handle before use. Anything ambiguous gets the same treatment.
- **A handle links to his post only when the forum is public.** The app forum is login-walled.
- **Don't run the same member two issues in a row** without raising it.

## Consent

- A public forum post is public. A **feedback-form message is not**: that reader has not agreed to
  appear in a mass email. Ask before quoting anyone from `feedback/` by handle.
- Being quoted is itself chizuk for the one quoted. A featured member wrote in to say so.

## The words themselves

- **A member's words run under his handle, so they are his.** Apply
  [01-sensitivities.md](01-sensitivities.md) and flag every line that trips it.
- **Keep the line that carries the mechanism, cut the line that carries a boast.** The nightly
  fantasy sentence is the post's whole point. The underwear sentence carries nothing and reads as a
  boast. Bracket the first, cut the second.
- **Bracket, don't bleep.** One explicit word blanks the whole paragraph for filtered readers.
- **Typos, spelling and nusach:** fix silently. **Anything that changes meaning:** flag, do not fix.
- **OPEN:** copy-editing a member's post that runs under his own handle has never been ruled on. It
  was done on Bereishis with the flag unanswered. Raise it each time until it is settled.

## A quote inside a quote

Members quote each other. When a reply quotes a third member, the attribution chain has to survive
the cut or the section has to be restructured. Flag it; do not flatten it silently. Honorifics a
member gives another member ("harav hagaon ... shlita", often jokingly) are his words, not ours.

## Placing

Unplaced material lives in `sources-inbox/` with a rating until an editor says which issue it runs
in. Once placed, the raw text moves to that issue's `sources/` folder. Rating is a recommendation
only; what runs is the editors' call ([08-working.md](08-working.md)).
