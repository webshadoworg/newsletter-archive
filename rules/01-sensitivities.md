# 01 Sensitivities

What must not go in front of a GYE reader, and how to say the thing that must.

Read this on every issue. The reader is a frum man who is fighting this, has probably fallen this
week, and opened the email anyway.

## The two tests

1. **Does this line help him, or does it feed him?** A description vivid enough to picture is a
   trigger however true it is.
2. **Does the sentence survive without the explicit word?** If yes, cut the word. If the whole point
   dies with it, the word stays and gets bracketed. That is the only reason to keep one.

## Explicitness

- **Write the softest wording that still carries the mechanism.** "Not mamash porn, but certainly
  soft porn" became "Not the worst stuff, but close to it."
- **Never name a site, a brand, a platform, a search term, or a workaround.** Not in a member's words
  either. A method a reader did not know is a gift to his yetzer hara.
- **No scene-setting on a fall.** The decision, the trigger and the aftermath are the story. The act
  is not. Where a member narrates one, run the decision and cut the walkthrough.
- **Filtered readers see a blank, not a bleep.** An explicit word gets the whole surrounding
  paragraph blanked by content filters, so a kept word must be bracketed. My own checks cannot see
  this; it has to be done by eye. See [04-members.md](04-members.md).
- **OPEN:** nobody has set the line for a member speaking in his own words. Two readers, two issues,
  unprompted: "sdny" on Sukkos said the forum is intense and triggering and the newsletter is "a more
  mellow version"; "yaakov" on Bereishis asked "if we should be a little less explicit." Until it is
  ruled, flag every candidate line and re-raise unanswered flags before send.

## Never say to a man who fell

- **No verdict on the reader.** The mekor's "a person would be turned corrupt by it" prints as "would
  be harmed by it." He is the person in the sentence.
- **No blame dressed as encouragement.** "You were given this test because you can pass it" reads as
  an accusation to someone who just failed it.
- **No demand for a feeling.** "Remorse should be done in a focused way" prints as "feel remorse as
  best you can." He cannot produce feeling on command.
- **No analogy to a lesser struggle.** Comparing this to lashon hara or a diet shrinks it.
- **No manufactured turning point.** A before-and-after frame invents a "before" that was never true.
  The honest limitation is the better hook.
- **No homework without the tool.** Do not ask him to list his triggers before the thing he is meant
  to pair them with exists.
- **No driving metaphors for a fall.** "Turning the car around" goes.

## Who else is reading

- **Wives read it.** Raised in the Re'eh feedback, never acted on. Assume a wife is reading any
  paragraph about a wife.
- **Men talking to men.** Never quote a handle that reads as a woman's name; attribute it as "one
  member" and drop the hyperlink, because the link exposes the username.
- **A reader who wrote to the feedback form has not agreed to appear in a mass email.** Ask before
  quoting anyone from `feedback/` by handle.

## Screening a source

The hazard is almost never a whole document. It is usually one paragraph sitting next to the
paragraph we want. Read the page around the quote before lifting it.

Hard stops, from the full screen in [`../teshuva-sources/harm-screen.md`](../teshuva-sources/harm-screen.md):
anything that names a physical consequence as a punishment for this aveirah, anything that puts the
reader's children or his health on the scale, anything describing the act, and anything whose only
use is to show how far the damage reaches. A source that works as a display rather than as a move the
reader can make this week does not go in.

A source must be **שווה לכל נפש**: is the person in it someone this reader can be, and is the move he
makes one this reader can make this week? "It sounds good on paper" is the warning sign, not a reason.

## When a flag goes unanswered

**A flag with no answer currently ships by default.** That is how both lines "yaakov" named went out.
Re-raise every unruled flag as one pick-one question before send.
